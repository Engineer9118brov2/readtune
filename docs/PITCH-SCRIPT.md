# ReadTune — pitch narration script

For the product demo video or a live presentation. It is not a feature of
readtune.tech and is not ReadTune's on-device read-aloud voice. The wording is
condensed from the hero copy (`index.html`) and the problem framing in
`docs/DEVPOST.md`.

**Target length:** ~30–40 seconds at a natural pace (about 90 words).

## Script

> Most reading tools hand you a pile of toggles and let you guess. ReadTune
> gives you a small preference check instead.
>
> A four-minute check tries two fonts, roomier spacing, and optional bionic
> anchors against the same calm starting page. Standard text appears twice on
> different passages, so the result has a steadier anchor. It suggests a setup
> to try, then carries it into articles, PDFs, and the web page already open.
>
> No account. Core reading stays on your device; optional AI and cloud voices
> clearly say when they send text. Free, open source, and built for the moment
> a page should feel possible again.
>
> This is ReadTune.

## Recording it

Use a calm, clear, moderate pace and add accurate captions to the demo video.
Do not present the narration as the extension's on-device Piper voice.
