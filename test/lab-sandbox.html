Optional

In Reader View, the "Ask AI" rail gives a Summary of the article or answers a question you type about it; select a hard passage and Simplify rewrites it in plainer words. No key, no account, ever — nothing runs until you ask. Simplify stays on-device only. Summary and typed questions run on your browser's built-in AI where it's ready, otherwise through ReadTune's free AI helper, which sends the article text — and, for a typed question, the question itself (it can be more personal than the article, so send only what you're comfortable sharing) — and may cache the result, so a popular article or question is generated once instead of per reader.
