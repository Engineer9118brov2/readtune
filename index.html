HOW IT WORKS

Find a starting setup in about four minutes.

The optional four-minute preference check starts with a warm-up and six short passages. Skip it and set things up yourself whenever you like.

1

Try a few settings

Read short passages with different fonts, spacing, or emphasis. Each passage changes one setting.

2

Get a suggestion

ReadTune compares your pace, comprehension, and comfort, then suggests settings worth trying.

3

Keep reading

Use your profile on articles, text-based PDFs, and the web pages you choose.

A suggestion, not a diagnosis. There is no single best setup for everyone. You can change any setting at any time.

READ YOUR WAY

Simple tools for reading and understanding.

Adjust the page

Change fonts, text size, line spacing, page width, and colour.

Keep your place

Use a reading ruler, focus on one paragraph or sentence, or let the page scroll.

Listen along

Read aloud with a natural voice that runs on your device, even offline. The text is highlighted as you listen.

Get help with the text

Ask for a summary, explanation, or definition. Simplify a passage or save a useful answer as a note.

Keep your place

Save your reading position, highlights, and notes. Revisit recent preference checks in Reading Lab.

Use tools only when you choose

Reader View, Restyle, and voice typing start when you click or use a shortcut. ReadTune does not need broad access to every site.

Talk to type

Dictate into text fields with spoken punctuation. Chrome handles speech recognition.

Also included: word lookup, syllable help, highlights and notes, speed reading, and voice typing.

PRIVACY

Private by default.

ReadTune does not need an account. Your reading profile and history stay on your device.

Read the privacy details
No account or tracking

No subscription, analytics, or telemetry in the extension.

Read-aloud stays on your device

The default voice works offline. Your reading text is not uploaded for it.

AI is optional

Opening AI sends nothing. If no on-device model is ready, an AI action you choose may send the relevant text to ReadTune’s relay.

Voice typing uses Chrome

Chrome sends microphone audio to Google for transcription when you dictate.
