Plain-language privacy

Your reading profile
stays yours.

The ReadTune extension is designed to work without an account, a server, or analytics. This page explains what the extension stores and when anything can leave your device. This website is a separate thing, and it is covered at the bottom.

What stays on your device

ReadTune stores your reading profile, calibration history, per-page reading position and highlights, the dyslexia-friendly-menus preference, and the sites where you explicitly enable automatic behavior. These are saved in Chrome's local extension storage, on your device. There is no ReadTune account and no ReadTune server.

Reader View, "Restyle this page", and PDF mode read the text of the page or file you are on so they can reformat it. That processing happens on your device. The page text is not sent to ReadTune or anywhere else.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses Piper, a neural voice that runs entirely on your device. The default voice ships inside the extension, so it works offline with nothing to download. If you pick one of the other voices in the Reading Lab, its model file is downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached on your device; the text you have read aloud is never uploaded.

If you choose the optional ElevenLabs voice, the passage you ask to hear and your own ElevenLabs API key are sent directly from your browser to ElevenLabs to generate audio. Your key is stored only in Chrome's local storage on your device. ReadTune has no server in this path and never receives your key or your text.

Talk to type (dictation)

If you use the dictation feature, ReadTune turns on Chrome's built-in speech recognition. Chrome sends the audio from your microphone to Google's speech service to transcribe it — this is the browser's own engine, not ReadTune's. The transcribed text is placed into the field you are typing in. ReadTune does not record, store, or transmit the audio or the transcript itself.

AI reading help

Reader View has two optional helpers: "Summary" (key points for the article) and "Simplify" (a plain-language rewrite of a passage you select). Where your browser provides on-device AI — Chrome's built-in Summarizer, Rewriter, or Prompt API — they run there: the model is downloaded and kept by Chrome, shared across every site, and neither the article nor your selection leaves your device. ReadTune adds no network access for this path.

If your browser has no on-device AI, you can add a free Google AI Studio (Gemini) key. Then the text you ask about — the article's opening for a summary, or the passage you selected for a rewrite — is sent, with your key, directly from your browser to Google's Gemini endpoint. Your key is stored only in Chrome's local storage on your device. ReadTune has no server in this path and never receives your key or your text. A rewrite can be wrong, so it is always shown beside the original, never in its place.

Permissions

  • Active tab and scripting: used only when you invoke ReadTune on the current tab — to open Reader View, restyle the page in place, or start dictation. Access ends when you navigate away.
  • Local storage: keeps your settings and progress on your device.
  • Optional site access: requested only if you turn on automatic Reader View or restyle for a specific site.
  • Optional network access (huggingface.co, api.elevenlabs.io, generativelanguage.googleapis.com): requested only when you choose an extra Piper voice, add an ElevenLabs key, or add a Gemini key, as described above.

What ReadTune does not do

The extension does not sell or share data, run analytics or telemetry, create an account, or send your browsing history to its creator. It does not use data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending. The extension is open source and can be inspected on GitHub.

This website

These marketing pages (the ones with readtune.app in the address bar) are hosted on Vercel and use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights so we can see which pages people read and how fast they load. It is cookieless, it does not follow you between sites, and it does not build a profile of you. It records the page URL and referrer, and derives coarse location (country), plus browser, operating system, and device type — never your name, email, or exact IP address, which Vercel discards after deriving those fields.

None of this runs inside the extension. Reader View, "Restyle this page", PDF mode, read-aloud, and dictation load no analytics of any kind, and nothing you read is ever visible to this website.
